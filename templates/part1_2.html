Finite Difference Operator

I convolved the cameraman image with Dx = [1, 0, -1] and its column form Dy, then computed magnitude = sqrt(dx² + dy²) and edges = magnitude > $threshold. Symmetric padding avoids artificial responses at the image border.

I tried thresholds $low_threshold, $threshold, and $high_threshold on the unscaled magnitude of the [0, 1] image. I chose $threshold: $low_threshold retains too much grass texture, while $high_threshold removes weaker parts of the camera and tripod contours. Dx and Dy share a signed display scale with gray at zero; the magnitude is scaled only for display.
